Dark Mode Hero

A Dark Mode Hero section uses a dark background with light text to create a modern and attractive design. It improves readability, reduces eye strain, and highlights important content. This section usually contains a heading, description, and buttons to capture user attention and provide a better user experience.

Centered Hero

A Centered Hero section places all content in the center of the webpage to create a clean and balanced layout. It helps users focus on the main message and improves the overall appearance of the website. This section usually includes a heading, description, and buttons for easy navigation. Centered Hero designs are commonly used in landing pages, portfolios, and business websites to provide a simple and attractive user experience.
